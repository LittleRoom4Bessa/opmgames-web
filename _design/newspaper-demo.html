Devlog — Project Zero

Week 1: a stick figure that already feels right

Nothing is drawn yet and everything moves. The first week of a prototype is the cheapest time to be wrong, so this one was spent entirely on jump arcs, coyote time, and the exact weight of a landing. Six tunings of gravity later, the placeholder capsule finally stopped feeling like a browser demo and started feeling like a character. The build is ugly. The build is honest.
